AUREQ 0.9.0-rc1 - macOS VST3 Installer
ASTRA Audio

EN
--
Double-click "AUREQ Installer.pkg" to install the AUREQ VST3 plug-in.

This installer places the plug-in at:
/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/AUREQ.vst3

After installation, open your DAW and rescan VST3 plug-ins.

Suggested DAW rescan locations:
- Reaper: Options > Preferences > Plug-ins > VST > Re-scan
- Ableton Live: Preferences > Plug-Ins > Rescan
- Bitwig Studio: Settings > Plug-ins > Rescan Plug-ins

This Release Candidate is not signed or notarized yet. macOS may show security warnings when opening the installer. This DMG is a visual distribution wrapper; the PKG remains the installer engine.

PT-BR

Clique duas vezes em "AUREQ Installer.pkg" para instalar o plug-in AUREQ VST3.

Este instalador coloca o plug-in em:
/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/AUREQ.vst3

Depois da instalacao, abra sua DAW e faca um novo scan dos plug-ins VST3.

Locais sugeridos para rescan:
- Reaper: Options > Preferences > Plug-ins > VST > Re-scan
- Ableton Live: Preferences > Plug-Ins > Rescan
- Bitwig Studio: Settings > Plug-ins > Rescan Plug-ins

Este Release Candidate ainda nao esta assinado nem notarizado. O macOS pode exibir avisos de seguranca ao abrir o instalador. Este DMG e uma embalagem visual de distribuicao; o PKG continua sendo o motor real de instalacao.
